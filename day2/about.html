About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple note-taking application designed to help you capture ideas quickly and keep track of important tasks.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Enter your note in the New note field.
  2. Click the Add Note button to save your note.
  3. Review your saved notes in the Your notes section.

Features

  • Quick and simple note creation
  • Easy-to-read list of saved notes
  • Keyboard shortcuts for faster navigation
  • Clean and accessible interface
